## Upload Checklist

1. Put all images directly inside this `images/` folder.
2. Keep the filenames lowercase and exactly the same as listed.
3. Do not add spaces in filenames.
4. Prefer landscape photos for banners, hero, event, and gallery images.
5. Prefer square photos for `principal-photo.jpg`, `chairman-photo.jpg`, and `manager-photo.jpg`.
